# Changelog

## 1.0.2, 2026-10-19

- Screenshots on GitHub and a full list of what MetalTyrian changes from OpenTyrian (Documentation/CHANGES.md).
- No more frantic tapping for the special weapon: quick specials (ready again within about a second, such as Pearl Wind, Ice Beam, Pulse Blast and Dual Vulcan) keep firing while Fire is held, and fire with autofire. Slow ones (Flare, MegaLaser, Missile Pod and the like) still need a tap. The controls panel says so.

## 1.0.1, 2026-10-18

- The Mac menu bar mirrors the game's Setup: Graphics, Sound and Controls menus. Command-F for full screen.
- The controls panel greets a new game once (the first level waits for fire) and otherwise shows only when paused.
- Single player throughout: the start menu is Mode (Full Game, Arcade, Time Attack); one list of saved games; one high score table; no two-player help or "Player 1" label.
- Timed Battle is now called Time Attack.
- Fixed: with main-gun autofire on, the special weapon fired once and never again; it now fires on each tap of Fire. The controls panel explains the special.
- Easier with the mouse: menu rows respond across their whole width and height; in the game and shop menus the highlight follows the mouse and one click acts (buying included).
- Default edge smoothing is Soft (Light stays Medium); new players start in full screen.
- A touch brighter, and Retro no longer darker than Sharp; effects unchanged.

## 1.0.0, 2026-10-18

First release.

- The whole of Tyrian 2000 for one player (five episodes, full game, arcade, time attack) running natively on macOS 13 or later on Apple Silicon. Two-player modes are left out in favour of single player.
- Metal display: Sharp or Retro (VGA scanlines) look, HDR glow, aspect and border choices, full screen.
- High-resolution title screen and repainted menu, shop and HUD pictures.
- Crisp Orbitron text throughout, or the original font.
- Edge smoothing (Off, Soft, Smooth) on the playfield's outlines.
- Dynamic light from shots, engines and explosions.
- Soft shadows: the playfield inset in the HUD, the score, lives and special floating over it.
- Music on Nuked OPL3; stereo effects placed by position; limiter.
- Mac controls for either hand: Space, Z, X, C with the arrows, or Space, /, ., , with W A S D, alongside a mouse, trackpad or gamepad; every key can be changed in Setup > Controls > Keys. Superbombs on their own key. Autofire for the main guns and sidekicks (1/2 or 0/9, or Setup > Controls). A controls panel when a new game starts (the first level waits for fire) and when paused.
- Mouse control (original or fly-to-pointer), adjustable speeds, momentum options.
- Arcade ship picker with the game's twelve ship looks.
- About box credits and bundled licences.
